Product range

Four core categories, one supply partner

High-Speed Ionic Hair Dryer

110,000 RPM brushless motor dries hair in half the time. Ionic output reduces frizz, and the 110-240V platform ships with US, EU, UK or AU plugs.

Professional Curling Iron

Ceramic-tourmaline barrel reaches working temperature in 30 seconds, with five settings from 80 to 230 degrees for every hair type.

Titanium Hair Straightener

Floating titanium plates with MCH heating deliver even heat in a single pass, cutting styling time and heat damage on thick or coarse hair.

Heated Straightening Brush

Heated bristles straighten while detangling. The fastest-growing SKU in salon retail, with anti-scald tips for consumer safety.

Frequently asked questions

What is your minimum order quantity?
500 pcs per model for OEM orders with your own logo and packaging. Mixed containers across up to four models are accepted.
Can you supply dual-voltage models for our market?
Yes. All four categories are available in 110-240V dual voltage, with US, EU, UK or AU plugs fitted to your order.
How long does sampling and mass production take?
Samples ship within 48 hours for standard models, 15-20 days for custom tooling. Mass production runs 25-30 days after deposit.
