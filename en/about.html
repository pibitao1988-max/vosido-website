About VOSIDO

A manufacturer, not a trading company

Brand Profile

Vosido is an integrated manufacturing and trading enterprise dedicated to the R&D, manufacturing, and global distribution of hair care appliances. Combining product design, mold development, mass production, quality inspection, and international trade under one roof, our core product range includes high-speed hair dryers, curling irons, and straightening flat irons, among other personal care electrical devices. Backed by a well-established industry chain and a stringent quality management system, Vosido has become a trusted hair care brand for consumers across many countries and regions worldwide.

The name “Vosido” fuses “Vision” and “Do” — anticipating market needs with foresight and honoring our quality commitment through action. We believe everyone deserves a professional-grade styling experience.

Manufacturing Strength

Vosido operates its own modern production base, featuring 8 automated assembly lines, an independent mold development workshop, and an electronics testing laboratory. From injection molding and PCB assembly to final assembly and testing, the entire process is self-controlled, ensuring every product — from components to finished goods — passes rigorous inspection.

  • Independent R&D: A professional team of structural and electronics engineers with full-process development capability from concept to mass production
  • Lean manufacturing: 8 automated lines with a daily capacity of 8,000 units, supporting flexible production and small-batch customization
  • Supply chain integration: Core components produced in-house, with long-term strategic partnerships with key component suppliers for dual assurance of cost and quality
  • Warehousing & logistics: Own 5,000㎡ finished-goods warehouse and partnerships with international logistics providers covering sea, air, and rail freight

Quality Assurance & Certifications

Quality is the foundation of Vosido. We have established a full-process quality control system spanning incoming material inspection (IQC), in-process inspection (IPQC), and outgoing inspection (OQC). Before leaving the factory, every product undergoes multiple checks including electrical safety tests, burn-in tests, and functional tests.

  • IPQC in-process patrol: Production line inspection every 2 hours, full inspection at key stations

  • Burn-in testing: Each product undergoes 4+ hours of high-temperature burn-in to eliminate early failures

OEM / ODM Services

Beyond our own brand, Vosido provides professional OEM (contract manufacturing) and ODM (design manufacturing) services to customers worldwide. Whether you are a cross-border e-commerce seller, a regional brand owner, or a chain retailer, we can offer flexible cooperation tailored to your needs.

  • OEM contract manufacturing: Production to customer-specified specs, with custom LOGO, packaging, and color schemes supported
  • ODM design: Differentiated product solutions from appearance design to function development
  • Flexible MOQ: Standard products from 500 units MOQ, customizable project MOQ negotiable
  • Fast sampling: Standard products sampled in 3–5 days, custom products in 7–15 days
  • Certification support: Assistance with product certification applications (CE/FCC/PSE, etc.)

Global Market Presence

Vosido products have been exported to over 50 countries and regions across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and South America. We have partners on major cross-border e-commerce platforms such as Amazon and AliExpress, while also providing stable supply to multiple international chain retailers.

  • European market: CE certified, covering Germany, France, the UK, Italy, and more
  • North American market: FCC certified, serving customers in the US and Canada
  • Asia-Pacific market: Covering Japan (PSE), South Korea, and multiple Southeast Asian countries
  • Middle East & South America: Rapidly growing markets such as the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Brazil, and Mexico
