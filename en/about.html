About VOSIDO

A manufacturer, not a trading company

VOSIDO has manufactured electrical hair styling tools since 2008, operating eight production lines dedicated to dryers, curling irons, straighteners and heated brushes. Our engineers work on motor efficiency, heating uniformity and thermal safety - the three things that decide whether a product survives its second year in a salon.

We supply distributors, salon chains and private-label brands in markets worldwide. Because we build in-house rather than broker, certification scope, tooling and packaging can all be adjusted before production rather than negotiated after.

Manufacturing capability

Eight production lines

Separate assembly lines for dryers and heated tools, with 2.4 million units annual capacity.

In-house testing lab

Thermal cycling, drop testing and electrical safety checks run on every production batch.

Tooling workshop

Mould fabrication and modification handled on site, shortening custom-project lead times.

Export documentation

Certificates of origin, test reports and market-specific compliance files prepared per shipment.

Milestones

  1. 2008 — Company founded, focused on hair styling tool manufacturing
  2. 2013 — Export programme launched for overseas distributors
  3. 2017 — Production expanded to eight dedicated lines
  4. 2021 — Annual capacity reached 2.4 million units
  5. 2026 — Brushless motor platform extended across the full dryer range
